Data Deletion — Number Generator

Developer: Mitrofan Games

A game account is created when you sign in by email (in the web version or by linking an email address in the app) and, in newer versions of the app, when you sign in to Google Play Games. The only data kept on our own server is the car serial number record, created if you won a car in an in-game event while signed in to Google Play Games or, in the web version, by email, a progress change check report, if the game marked your profile, and the game account with a copy of your progress, if you have one (see the privacy policy). We remove their link to you on request. All other data is stored on your device or by Google and Yandex, and you can delete it yourself — see below.

How to delete your account

If you have the game, delete the account in it: Settings → “Documents” → “Account and data deletion”. If in your version of the game this button opens this page, update the game. If you just want to start the game over, its settings have “Reset progress” — it deletes nothing on the server.

Without the game, you can delete the account here: sign in the same way you do in the game — that is how we know the account is yours.

What is deleted

The game account — the email address, sign-in keys, the copy of your progress on the server and its versions, progress set aside when accounts were merged, gifts, promo codes and the phone key information — is deleted entirely 7 days after the request. After deletion only a hash of the sign-in identifier (the email address or the Google Play Games profile) is kept, for 90 days, so that the game on your devices starts over and does not put the deleted progress back on the server; then it is deleted too.

The car serial number record on our server holds your gamer profile identifier, the event, the copy number, and when it was assigned. We delete the profile identifier, after which the record can no longer be linked to you. What is kept is an anonymous “event — number — time” line, so that no other player gets the same number. Without a request, the record is kept for as long as the copy number exists.

A progress change check report (profile identifier, marks, game version, platform, balance, currency, number of spins, record of a lifted mark) is deleted entirely. Without a request it is deleted automatically one year after the last report.

Data you can delete yourself

  • Game progress on your phone — uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Google Play Games cloud save, achievements and scores — stored by Google; delete them in your Google Play Games profile.
  • Advertising ID — reset or delete it in your device settings (Google → Ads). Yandex advertising and analytics data (Yandex Mobile Ads, AppMetrica) is not linked to an account and is kept under the Yandex Privacy Policy.
  • Web version (play.mitrofanof.ru) — progress lives in your browser (clear the site's data in your browser settings); if you signed in by email, it is also on our server, and you can delete it above.
