Data Deletion — Number Generator

Developer: Mitrofan Games

Number Generator has no user accounts. The only data kept on our own server is the car serial number record, created if you won a car in an in-game event while signed in to Google Play Games, and a progress change check report, if the game marked your profile (see the privacy policy). We remove their link to you on request. All other data is stored on your device or by Google and Yandex, and you can delete it yourself — see below.

How to request deletion

  • 1. Email mr.aksi.lc@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion — Number Generator”.
  • 2. Include your car's copy number shown in the garage (for example, “Copy #17”) and, if you can, a screenshot of the garage. For a progress change check report, include the ID the game shows in the “Progress modified” window.
  • 3. We delete the data within 30 days and reply to your email.

What is deleted

The record on our server holds your Google Play Games profile identifier, the event, the copy number, and when it was assigned. On request we delete the profile identifier, after which the record can no longer be linked to you.

What is kept is an anonymous “event — number — time” line, so that no other player gets the same number. The car and its number stay in your game (they are in your save), but after a reinstall without cloud save the old number cannot be restored. Without a request, the record is kept for as long as the copy number exists.

A progress change check report (profile identifier, marks, game version, platform, balance, currency, number of spins, record of a lifted mark) is deleted entirely. Without a request it is deleted automatically one year after the last report. If the mark is still on in your game, the game will send a report again the next time you sign in to Google Play Games.

Data you can delete yourself

  • Game progress on your phone — uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Google Play Games cloud save, achievements and scores — stored by Google; delete them in your Google Play Games profile.
  • Advertising ID — reset or delete it in your device settings (Google → Ads). Yandex advertising and analytics data (Yandex Mobile Ads, AppMetrica) is not linked to an account and is kept under the Yandex Privacy Policy.
  • Web version (play.mitrofanof.ru) — progress lives only in your browser; clear the site's data in your browser settings.
