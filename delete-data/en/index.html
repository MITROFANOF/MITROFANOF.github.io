Data Deletion — Number Generator

Developer: Mitrofan Games

A game account is created when you sign in by email (in the web version or by linking an email address in the app) and, in newer versions of the app, when you sign in to Google Play Games. The only data kept on our own server is the car serial number record, created if you won a car in an in-game event while signed in to Google Play Games or, in the web version, by email, a progress change check report, if the game marked your profile, and the game account with a copy of your progress, if you have one (see the privacy policy). We remove their link to you on request. All other data is stored on your device or by Google and Yandex, and you can delete it yourself — see below.

How to request deletion

The “Delete account and data” button in the game deletes nothing by itself — it opens this page. If you just want to start the game over, its settings have “Reset progress”.

Fill in what you have: this is how we find you on our server. If you have none of these, our server holds no data about you — the rest you can delete yourself, see below.

Your email app will open with the email ready — check it, attach the screenshot if you gave an ID or a car number, and send it. If it did not open, send the email yourself:

To
mr.aksi.lc@gmail.com
Subject
Data deletion — Number Generator

    

  

  

  
We delete the data within 30 days and reply to your email. An email without any of these cannot be linked to any player, and we delete nothing on it.


  
What is deleted

  
The record on our server holds your Google Play Games profile identifier, the event, the copy number, and when it was assigned. On request we delete the profile identifier, after which the record can no longer be linked to you.

  
What is kept is an anonymous “event — number — time” line, so that no other player gets the same number. The car and its number stay in your game (they are in your save), but after a reinstall without cloud save the old number cannot be restored. Without a request, the record is kept for as long as the copy number exists.

  
A progress change check report (profile identifier, marks, game version, platform, balance, currency, number of spins, record of a lifted mark) is deleted entirely. Without a request it is deleted automatically one year after the last report. If the mark is still on in your game, the game will send a report again the next time you sign in to Google Play Games.

  
A game account — the email address, sign-in keys, the copy of your progress on the server, and progress set aside when accounts were merged are deleted entirely. If you only sign in through Google Play Games and have not linked an email address, include the support ID from the game's settings.
