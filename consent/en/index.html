Consent to the processing of personal data — Number Generator

Version 2026-10-10

By ticking “I consent to the processing of my email address” in the sign-in form of the Number Generator web version (play.mitrofanof.ru), you consent to the operator — Ivan Sergeevich Mitrofanov, the game's developer (mr.aksi.lc@gmail.com) — processing your personal data on the following terms.

  • 1. Data: your email address.
  • 2. Purpose: signing in to the game with a code sent by email, and keeping a copy of your game progress on the server so that you can continue playing on another device or in another browser; the operator looking at the contents of that copy (plates, money, cars) to answer support requests, restore progress, check for unfair play, and tune the game's balance.
  • 3. Actions: collection, recording, storage, use (sending the email with the sign-in code, looking at the saved progress), and deletion. Your email address is not shared with third parties other than the processor named below and is not used for newsletters or other mailings.
  • 4. Processor: Yandex.Cloud LLC (Yandex Cloud) — data storage and email delivery, servers in Russia.
  • 5. Term: until the account is deleted or consent is withdrawn.
  • 6. Withdrawing consent: email mr.aksi.lc@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion — Number Generator”; the account and the save are deleted within 30 days.
