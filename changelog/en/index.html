What’s new — License Plate Generator

Changelog

Version 3.2.1 — October 2026

Everything that changed since version 3.1.0.

New

  • Car Week, October 19–25 — daily event tasks with an exclusive car as the prize.
  • Garage — the car you win stands in a 3D garage; orbit and zoom the camera.
  • Your plate on the car — try any plate from your collection on it and install it. The first install is free, swaps are for an ad.
  • Car award cinematic.
  • Car serial number — “Unit No. N” in order among all winners.
  • Google Play achievement for the event car.
  • Graphics setting (Auto / Economy / High) for low-end phones.
  • Military plates — 38 units and agencies, priced by the unit’s prestige.
  • Redesigned Belarusian plates — the real typeface, green BY, the flag, the letter I and Minsk’s second code (8).
  • 60+ new special series of administrations and law-enforcement agencies, plus the ННН series (region 32).
  • Illegal region codes — 12 never-issued codes are marked “Illegal”: a black card, a notice and a Google Play achievement.
  • Look-alike symbols combo — B and 8, O and 0, A and 4, E and 3, T and 7, I and 1, in a row or scattered.
  • Mirrored digits with the region — a plate like 767·67 now counts.
  • Google Play cloud save, with a choice of version when the phone and the cloud differ.
  • Collection up to 10,000 slots.
  • Save protection against hand edits — edited saves stay off the leaderboard.
  • Skip the animation of Epic and Legendary plates (in settings).
  • Frame rate setting — 30 to 144, 60 by default.
  • Google Play achievements and leaderboard tiles in the menu.
  • Telegram news tile.

Fixes

  • Fast taps are no longer missed: the 10-spins-a-second cap is gone (now 25 a second with headroom).
  • Auto-sell no longer stutters on large collections.
  • Less stutter on rapid spins: the game writes its save less often.
  • Smoother on Android: the game used to run at 30 frames per second.
  • Google Play achievements and leaderboard scores are now actually sent.
  • A failing Google Play Games sign-in no longer gets in the way of launching.
  • Police and taxi plates no longer roll with a foreign region.
  • The “Unlocked” notice names the rarity in the player’s language.
  • Landscape: taps on the side margins spin, and the badge no longer opens the card by accident.
  • The interface stays clear of the camera cutout and the gesture bar.
  • The rarity catalog names Belarus’s regions instead of printing codes.
  • Updated Yandex ad library.
  • Smaller download: menu pictures are compressed.
  • Rarity thresholds and prices recalibrated for the new combos.
