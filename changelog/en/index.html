What’s new — License Plate Generator

Changelog

Version 3.5.0 — October 2026

Everything that changed since version 3.4.0.

Auto-clicker

  • Auto-clicker — bought in Upgrades for dollars: 1 to 25 spins a second, the first rung costs 100 $.
  • The “Auto” switch on the home screen turns it on and shows its speed (“Auto 7/s”). The screen stays on while it runs.
  • When the game waits for your answer in a window, the auto-clicker turns itself off.

Plates

  • Legendary and Epic plates reveal symbol by symbol, each one lighting up a moment after the last. The ceremony can be skipped, and Settings has “Show the plate at once”.
  • A “T1…T9” badge marks the game’s most valuable plates: the nine highest prices of each plate type. The place shows on the drop, on the plate’s card, in the collection and in the safe.
  • Plate buyback — a new menu tile: trade all the Legendary plates in your collection for dollars at once, at the same rate. Plates in the safe are left alone.
  • A “Favourite plates” tile in the menu.

Series

  • ААА 77 and ААА 99 are now departmental series, like ООО 77.
  • Number 001 (and 0001 in Belarus) is worth more: the small-number rule counts twice for a one.

Leaderboard: Season 2

  • From November 1, 00:00 Moscow time — a new leaderboard with a clean slate: the season’s spins and dearest plate count from the season’s start, and only in version 3.5.0 and later.
  • Two separate lists — “Android” and “Browser” — with three tables: “Generated”, “Dearest plate” and the event car’s “Copies”.
  • Until the season starts, Android opens the familiar Google Play Games leaderboard.

Levels and the event

  • A new level’s reward flashes on the balance and rises out of it after the level card — in the silver and gold themes in the theme’s own colour.
  • A level reached during a rare plate’s ceremony is shown after it instead of getting lost beneath it.
  • Event tasks open at midnight Moscow time, whatever the phone’s time zone.
  • The car’s copy number reads #N on Android and №N in the browser.

Saves

  • Every Google Play account has its own progress, even on one phone.
  • “Start over” on a new install no longer replaces the account’s progress: the cloud is switched off on that device, and Settings turns it back on.
  • The cloud window says plainly that the chosen progress replaces the account’s progress on every device.
  • Progress goes up to the cloud when you leave the game, every 5 minutes and right after a Legendary plate. “No connection” on mobile data is rarer: a dropped request is retried by itself.
  • Saves reach the disk safely — progress is not lost if the phone powers off right after a spin.
  • Older versions of the game on a second device or in the browser no longer overwrite new progress; they ask to update instead.

Fixes

  • Auto-sell no longer slows the game down in long sessions.
  • The menu runs faster on budget phones.
  • Settings labels are readable in the gold and silver themes.
  • Email sign-in with a Google Play account’s address explains why that progress opens only through Google Play Games.
  • The progress reset button moved to the very bottom of Settings; “Account and data deletion” leads to the request page.

Web version

  • The leaderboard and achievements in the browser too.
  • The auto-clicker keeps the screen on in the browser too.

Version 3.4.0 — October 2026

Everything that changed since version 3.3.1.

Levels and rewards

  • Level rewards — every new level pays 10,000 ₽ × the level², every 10th also 5 $ × the level.
  • Levels reached before the update are paid once at first launch.
  • Levels now go up to 500. The spin stake stops rising at level 60.

Plates

  • Favourite plates — up to 5 slots, each holds one exact plate. When it drops it goes straight to the safe — even a full one.
  • Trade a Legendary plate for dollars: 1 $ for every 10,000,000 ₽ of its price.
  • Auto-sell: switch each tier on separately, Epic included; the top price limit goes up to 10,000,000 ₽.
  • Military plates now drop under a bet on any region.
  • Region picker: search by code or name, each region shows its price multiplier.
  • The catalog shows the tier chances for the chosen region.

Series

  • New: СКР 197 and 199 (Investigative Committee), МУР 77, СНМ 152 (police), the Nizhny Novgorod prosecutor's office (А001–А100 МР 252, С001–С015 НМ 252), РУС 125, status numbers 009, 011, 013 and 209 in Chechnya (95).
  • Pricier: КРА 95 and АМР 97.
  • АНН 152 is no longer a special series — it is an ordinary civil series issued to everyone in 2011. If you own such plates, the game offers to sell them at the old price.

Collection and stats

  • "Today" tab: today's spins, record, tiers and combinations.
  • A 30-day spins chart on the "General" tab.
  • "Special" filter: duplicates, illegal and no-flag plates — in the collection and the safe.
  • Your balance in the header of the collection, safe, upgrades and auto-sell; the collection value on one line under the title.
  • Redesigned sell dialogs.

Saves

  • Backups: the game keeps a copy of your progress every day. Restore it in Settings → "Backups".
  • The cloud save never replaces the progress on your phone without asking. If the cloud holds fewer spins, the game asks twice.
  • An Epic or Legendary plate is saved the moment it drops.
  • The cloud save moved from Google Play to our own server (your progress moves by itself), which also keeps copies from the last few days: if something goes missing, support can bring it back.
  • Link an email in Settings to open the same progress in the browser (play.mitrofanof.ru) and on iPhone.

Other

  • Rewarded ads now work outside Russia and the CIS: selling without the fee, replacing tasks, the daily ad, the garage plate swap and the event's missed day are open to players in other countries.
  • The first ad loads right at launch, so "for an ad" buttons don't keep you waiting.
  • Promo codes in Settings.
  • A "New update" window when a newer version is out in the store.
  • Settings show the game's version and a "Documents" section: terms of use, privacy policy, account and data deletion.
  • The level-up notice says what the level paid.

Fixes

  • Daily tasks no longer ask for the internet when it is there.
  • The cloud save no longer says "No connection" right after launch when there is one.
  • When an ad is closed early, the game says no reward was paid.
  • The "Duplicate" mark stays after the original is sold.
  • Mouse scrolling in Google Play Games on PC.
  • False protection alerts for honest players.

Web version

  • The car week and the garage in the browser too. The winner gets the serial number of the exclusive car (email sign-in needed).
  • With the game open in two tabs only the latest saves, and progress is never doubled.

Version 3.3.1 — October 2026

Plates

  • Plates no longer repeat — a spin never gives a plate you already have: in the collection, the safe or on a car. A plate without the flag and the same plate with it count as different.
  • Copies collected earlier wear a "Duplicate" ribbon. Sorted by date they keep their place; in every other sort they sit right below the original.
  • Once at launch the game shows how many copies you hold and offers to move them to the safe.

Web version

  • Sign in by email at play.mitrofanof.ru: a code arrives by mail, progress is kept on the server and survives clearing the browser — carry on from another device.
  • With the game open in several tabs only the latest saves; the others say so.

Fixes

  • A black or purple screen at launch on some phones.
  • The collection search keyboard did not open again until a restart.
  • Daily tasks got stuck if the phone's date had once jumped far ahead.
  • A slow network answer could cause a false "progress changed" mark.
  • The collection header now reads "Collection value".
  • Saves are better protected.

Version 3.3.0 — October 2026

Everything that changed since version 3.2.2.

New

  • “Full set” combination — all digits and all letters alike: X555XX, T777TT. These plates are as rare as Legendaries and are now priced like it. Counts on civil, military and Belarusian plates, not on taxis.
  • Plates without the flag — 5% of Russian civil plates drop without the tricolour and are worth 5% more; the bonus shows in the region chip.
  • “Prices have changed” — if an update made your plates cheaper, on first launch the game offers to sell them at the old price or keep them.
  • Search in the collection and the safe — letters find a series (“amr”), digits find the number or the region. A–Z sort, and your chosen sort is now remembered.
  • “Ad of the day” in the tasks — +$50 once a day, separate from the all-tasks bonus.
  • A new home screen — the background glows in the tier’s colour, the plate is whiter, with a soft gloss and shadow.
  • “Silver Mono” theme — silver with a monochrome glow. Unlocks together with “Silver”.
  • The safe grows to 100 slots (was 50).
  • A support ID in the settings. If the game mistakenly marked your progress as modified, write to us with this ID and we will remove the mark. Gifts from the developer now arrive right in the game, too.

Prices

  • Epic plates are worth more — the Epic multiplier climbs smoothly from ×5 to ×50 as a plate nears the Legendary threshold. Before, plates one point apart could differ tenfold in price.
  • Generation cost depends on your level — 1,000 ₽ at the start and +50 ₽ per level: 3,000 ₽ at level 40, 4,000 ₽ at level 60 (it was 3,000 ₽ for everyone). The level cap went up from 50 to 60. Belarusian plates cost ×0.71 and pay back like Russian ones.

Series

  • 31 new series: 15 departmental — ОУУ 64, ААА 57, ССС 97, УУУ 61, УТУ 61, ОРР 47 and 78, ОТТ 47, МАЕ 47, ООО 47 and 66, СОО 66, ОВО 78, ООМ 54, ОАО 57 — and 16 Chechen ones, КРА 95 among them.
  • АМР 97: FSB and Interior Ministry numbers are now worth as much as other departments’, the top officials’ А001–А009 МР 97 are worth more than any other individual’s number, and А488МР97 and А901МР97 are now government reserve plates.
  • АМР 97 numbers that were mistakenly counted in two series at once now count one and are worth about half as much. If you own one, the game offers to sell it at the old price.
  • ЕКХ — the FSO series in regions 77, 93, 97, 99 and 177 — is now worth as much as other departmental series, and АМР 77 is a series of its own: these plates are worth more. ЕКХ 95 is a Chechen series of its own.
  • АМР 150 plates now count one series — the Moscow Oblast police directorate. They were also counted as a prestige plate by mistake, which made them far pricier; their price and rarity are now corrected.
  • ССС 23 plates were mistakenly worth twice as much as other Krasnodar prestige series — their price is fixed.
  • Region 95: with the new series, Legendaries there dropped ten times more often than intended, so the tier thresholds were raised. Some of the region’s plates got cheaper — sell them at the old price in the “Prices have changed” window.

Stats

  • The record is priced by today’s rules, and shows your dearest plate if you own one worth more.
  • The “Prestige plate” row counts every prestige plate again.
  • Russian and Belarusian civil plates get separate rows.

Fixes and protection

  • The Google Play cloud save is now protected like the save on your phone. If you play on several devices, update the game on all of them: older versions can’t read the new cloud save.
  • A damaged plate in the collection no longer stops the game from starting.
  • Stronger protection against cheats.
  • Plates no longer draw inside a black box on older phones.
  • Smoother scrolling on 120 Hz screens.
  • Selling for an ad shows “Ad unavailable” less often.
  • The game keeps three daily copies of your save and restores it by itself if the file gets damaged.
  • New daily tasks arrive only when you are online.
  • The launch screen is dark instead of grey.

Version 3.2.2 — October 2026

Fixes

  • Legendaries are back. In 3.2.1 some Legendary plates dropped to Epic: a new combination pushed the tier thresholds up. The thresholds are back to 3.1.0, so 95% of those plates are Legendary again, and the new series and combinations add even more Legendaries.
  • “Look-alike symbols” now count only when the whole plate is one symbol: B888BB, T777TT 777.
  • Tier thresholds never go up anymore — a plate in your collection won’t lose its tier after an update.

Thanks for your reviews — they helped us find the problem.
