What’s new — License Plate Generator

Changelog

Version 3.3.1 — October 2026

Plates

  • Plates no longer repeat — a spin never gives a plate you already have: in the collection, the safe or on a car. A plate without the flag and the same plate with it count as different.
  • Copies collected earlier wear a "Duplicate" ribbon. Sorted by date they keep their place; in every other sort they sit right below the original.
  • Once at launch the game shows how many copies you hold and offers to move them to the safe.

Web version

  • Sign in by email at play.mitrofanof.ru: a code arrives by mail, progress is kept on the server and survives clearing the browser — carry on from another device.
  • With the game open in several tabs only the latest saves; the others say so.

Fixes

  • A black or purple screen at launch on some phones.
  • The collection search keyboard did not open again until a restart.
  • Daily tasks got stuck if the phone's date had once jumped far ahead.
  • A slow network answer could cause a false "progress changed" mark.
  • The collection header now reads "Collection value".
  • Saves are better protected.

Version 3.3.0 — October 2026

Everything that changed since version 3.2.2.

New

  • “Full set” combination — all digits and all letters alike: X555XX, T777TT. These plates are as rare as Legendaries and are now priced like it. Counts on civil, military and Belarusian plates, not on taxis.
  • Plates without the flag — 5% of Russian civil plates drop without the tricolour and are worth 5% more; the bonus shows in the region chip.
  • “Prices have changed” — if an update made your plates cheaper, on first launch the game offers to sell them at the old price or keep them.
  • Search in the collection and the safe — letters find a series (“amr”), digits find the number or the region. A–Z sort, and your chosen sort is now remembered.
  • “Ad of the day” in the tasks — +$50 once a day, separate from the all-tasks bonus.
  • A new home screen — the background glows in the tier’s colour, the plate is whiter, with a soft gloss and shadow.
  • “Silver Mono” theme — silver with a monochrome glow. Unlocks together with “Silver”.
  • The safe grows to 100 slots (was 50).
  • A support ID in the settings. If the game mistakenly marked your progress as modified, write to us with this ID and we will remove the mark. Gifts from the developer now arrive right in the game, too.

Prices

  • Epic plates are worth more — the Epic multiplier climbs smoothly from ×5 to ×50 as a plate nears the Legendary threshold. Before, plates one point apart could differ tenfold in price.
  • Generation cost depends on your level — 1,000 ₽ at the start and +50 ₽ per level: 3,000 ₽ at level 40, 4,000 ₽ at level 60 (it was 3,000 ₽ for everyone). The level cap went up from 50 to 60. Belarusian plates cost ×0.71 and pay back like Russian ones.

Series

  • 31 new series: 15 departmental — ОУУ 64, ААА 57, ССС 97, УУУ 61, УТУ 61, ОРР 47 and 78, ОТТ 47, МАЕ 47, ООО 47 and 66, СОО 66, ОВО 78, ООМ 54, ОАО 57 — and 16 Chechen ones, КРА 95 among them.
  • АМР 97: FSB and Interior Ministry numbers are now worth as much as other departments’, the top officials’ А001–А009 МР 97 are worth more than any other individual’s number, and А488МР97 and А901МР97 are now government reserve plates.
  • АМР 97 numbers that were mistakenly counted in two series at once now count one and are worth about half as much. If you own one, the game offers to sell it at the old price.
  • ЕКХ — the FSO series in regions 77, 93, 97, 99 and 177 — is now worth as much as other departmental series, and АМР 77 is a series of its own: these plates are worth more. ЕКХ 95 is a Chechen series of its own.
  • АМР 150 plates now count one series — the Moscow Oblast police directorate. They were also counted as a prestige plate by mistake, which made them far pricier; their price and rarity are now corrected.
  • ССС 23 plates were mistakenly worth twice as much as other Krasnodar prestige series — their price is fixed.
  • Region 95: with the new series, Legendaries there dropped ten times more often than intended, so the tier thresholds were raised. Some of the region’s plates got cheaper — sell them at the old price in the “Prices have changed” window.

Stats

  • The record is priced by today’s rules, and shows your dearest plate if you own one worth more.
  • The “Prestige plate” row counts every prestige plate again.
  • Russian and Belarusian civil plates get separate rows.

Fixes and protection

  • The Google Play cloud save is now protected like the save on your phone. If you play on several devices, update the game on all of them: older versions can’t read the new cloud save.
  • A damaged plate in the collection no longer stops the game from starting.
  • Stronger protection against cheats.
  • Plates no longer draw inside a black box on older phones.
  • Smoother scrolling on 120 Hz screens.
  • Selling for an ad shows “Ad unavailable” less often.
  • The game keeps three daily copies of your save and restores it by itself if the file gets damaged.
  • New daily tasks arrive only when you are online.
  • The launch screen is dark instead of grey.

Version 3.2.2 — October 2026

Fixes

  • Legendaries are back. In 3.2.1 some Legendary plates dropped to Epic: a new combination pushed the tier thresholds up. The thresholds are back to 3.1.0, so 95% of those plates are Legendary again, and the new series and combinations add even more Legendaries.
  • “Look-alike symbols” now count only when the whole plate is one symbol: B888BB, T777TT 777.
  • Tier thresholds never go up anymore — a plate in your collection won’t lose its tier after an update.

Thanks for your reviews — they helped us find the problem.

Version 3.2.1 — October 2026

Everything that changed since version 3.1.0.

New

  • Car Week, October 19–25 — daily event tasks with an exclusive car as the prize.
  • Garage — the car you win stands in a 3D garage; orbit and zoom the camera.
  • Your plate on the car — try any plate from your collection on it and install it. The first install is free, swaps are for an ad.
  • Car award cinematic.
  • Car serial number — “Unit No. N” in order among all winners.
  • Google Play achievement for the event car.
  • Graphics setting (Auto / Economy / High) for low-end phones.
  • Military plates — 38 units and agencies, priced by the unit’s prestige.
  • Redesigned Belarusian plates — the real typeface, green BY, the flag, the letter I and Minsk’s second code (8).
  • 60+ new special series of administrations and law-enforcement agencies, plus the ННН series (region 32).
  • Illegal region codes — 12 never-issued codes are marked “Illegal”: a black card, a notice and a Google Play achievement.
  • Look-alike symbols combo — B and 8, O and 0, A and 4, E and 3, T and 7, I and 1, in a row or scattered.
  • Mirrored digits with the region — a plate like 767·67 now counts.
  • Google Play cloud save, with a choice of version when the phone and the cloud differ.
  • Collection up to 10,000 slots.
  • Save protection against hand edits — edited saves stay off the leaderboard.
  • Skip the animation of Epic and Legendary plates (in settings).
  • Frame rate setting — 30 to 144, 60 by default.
  • Google Play achievements and leaderboard tiles in the menu.
  • Telegram news tile.

Fixes

  • Fast taps are no longer missed: the 10-spins-a-second cap is gone (now 25 a second with headroom).
  • Auto-sell no longer stutters on large collections.
  • Less stutter on rapid spins: the game writes its save less often.
  • Smoother on Android: the game used to run at 30 frames per second.
  • Google Play achievements and leaderboard scores are now actually sent.
  • A failing Google Play Games sign-in no longer gets in the way of launching.
  • Police and taxi plates no longer roll with a foreign region.
  • The “Unlocked” notice names the rarity in the player’s language.
  • Landscape: taps on the side margins spin, and the badge no longer opens the card by accident.
  • The interface stays clear of the camera cutout and the gesture bar.
  • The rarity catalog names Belarus’s regions instead of printing codes.
  • Updated Yandex ad library.
  • Smaller download: menu pictures are compressed.
  • Rarity thresholds and prices recalibrated for the new combos.
